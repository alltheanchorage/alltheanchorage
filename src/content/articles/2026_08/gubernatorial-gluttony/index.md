In an effort to get out the vote for the upcoming August Primary Election, Wild Scoops has released 17 new flavors as part of their ‘Gubernatorial Gluttony' line. With a flavor for each gubernatorial candidate, the local ice cream lover has a lot to choose from. The flavors are listed below in the order that they’ll appear on your ballot.

Bernadette “Butter Pecan” Wilson (Registered Republican)<br>
Tom “Baked Alaska” Begich (Registered Democrat)<br>
Click “Nesquick” Bishop (Registered Republican)<br>
Dave “Blueberry” Bronson (Registered Republican)<br>
Rhubarb Adam Crum Crumble (Registered Republican)<br>
Edna “French Fries” DeVries (Registered Republican)<br>
M&Meda DeWitt (Nonpartisan)<br>
Michael “Moon Pie” Gilbert (Registered Republican)<br>
Matt “Lollipop” Heilala (Registered Republican)<br>
Shelley “Hazelnut” Hughes (Registered Republican)<br>
Jonathan “Krispy Kreme” Kreiss-Tomkins (Registered Democrat)<br>
Hank “Hersheys” Kroll (Registered Republican)<br>
Lesil “Milky Way” McGuire (Registered Republican)<br>
James “Peach Cobbler” Parkin (Registered Republican)<br>
Destry “Pink Lemonade” Payne Sr. (Undeclared)<br>
Treg “Tree Nut” Taylor (Registered Republican)<br>
Bill “Wonka Bar” Walker (Nonpartisan)

Wild Scoops is offering a flight of all 17 flavors dubbed the “All Perspective Collective”. A hungry customer in line stated that they were going to cast their vote for whichever candidate had the best flavor.
