In an effort to get out the vote for the upcoming August Primary Election, Wild Scoops has released 17 new flavors as part of their ‘Gubernatorial Gluttony' line. With a flavor for each gubernatorial candidate, the local ice cream lover has a lot to choose from. The flavors are listed below in the order that they’ll appear on your ballot.

Bernadette “Butter Pecan” Wilson (Registered Republican)
Tom “Baked Alaska” Begich (Registered Democrat)
Click “Nesquick” Bishop (Registered Republican)
Dave “Blueberry” Bronson (Registered Republican)
Rhubarb Adam Crum Crumble (Registered Republican)
Edna “French Fries” DeVries (Registered Republican)
M&Meda DeWitt (Nonpartisan)
Michael “Moon Pie” Gilbert (Registered Republican)
Matt “Lollipop” Heilala (Registered Republican)
Shelley “Hazelnut” Hughes (Registered Republican)
Jonathan “Krispy Kreme” Kreiss-Tomkins (Registered Democrat)
Hank “Hersheys” Kroll (Registered Republican)
Lesil “Milky Way” McGuire (Registered Republican)
James “Peach Cobbler” Parkin (Registered Republican)
Destry “Pink Lemonade” Payne Sr. (Undeclared)
Treg “Tree Nut” Taylor (Registered Republican)
Bill “Wonka Bar” Walker (Nonpartisan)

Wild Scoops is offering a flight of all 17 flavors dubbed the “All Perspective Collective”. A hungry customer in line stated that they were going to cast their vote for whichever candidate had the best flavor.
