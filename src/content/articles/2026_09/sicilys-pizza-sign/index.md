Sources report that the Cook Inlet natural gas shortage has caused Sicily’s Pizza to decommission another letter in their neon sign, bringing the operational letters down to just ‘Y PIZZA’. “Due to the Cook Inlet natural gas shortage and the subsequent rising cost of electricity, we have made the difficult decision to retire another letter in the neon sign at our location on Northern Lights,” stated the pizza business in a press release. “We have proudly served affordable pizza to Anchorage residents for over 25 years, first as ‘SICILY’S PIZZA’, then as ‘ICILY’S PIZZA’, then as ‘IILY’S PIZZA’, then as ‘ILY’S PIZZA’, then as ‘LY’S PIZZA’, and most recently as ‘Y’S PIZZA’, and we are looking forward to continuing cooking up delicious pies as ‘Y PIZZA’ for the next 25 years, or at least until the next energy crisis.”

“In this energy-scarce environment, we obviously chose to prioritize the quality of our pizzas over the letters in our neon sign, and thus had to cut off power to the apostrophe and the ‘S’ to keep our budget balanced,” continued the press release. “No, a new business named ‘Y PIZZA’ has not moved into town! It’s the same old establishment with the same pizza you love, just with slightly different branding. If Anchorage comes up with a cheap alternative energy source, we will consider turning a letter back on.”

At press time, the neon sign outside Club Paris was guzzling enough electricity to power an entire city block.
