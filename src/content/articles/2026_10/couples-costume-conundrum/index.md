Dear Calling Nome,

I've been seeing someone for a while now and we thought it would be fun to dress up in a couple's costumes for Halloween. However, this decision is turning out to be fraught with tension, as I adhere to a very strict “must be a scary costume” rule, meanwhile she keeps suggesting truly insane ideas like the Muppets or Ken and Barbie. How can I stay true to the spirit of the season while keeping our relationship intact?

Scared silly in Soldotna

----------

Dear Scared,

Well, if this isn't the classic autumnal relationship crisis - the joint Halloween costume. I recall one couple that refused to compromise on their costumes and ended up as Indiana Jones and a Ghostbuster (I’d watch that!).

Choosing between making your partner happy and picking the perfect Halloween costume is a tricky dilemma. In your case, I would try to walk the fine line of costume compromise and go with a terrifying costume for you and a mild one for her. Suggestions from readers include: Big Bad Wolf and Little Red Riding Hood, Zombie Ken and regular Barbie, and Decapitated Kermit the Frog and Miss Piggy. The opportunities for creativity, relationship growth, and costumes that make onlookers go “huh?” are endless!

Stay spooky, Calling Nome
