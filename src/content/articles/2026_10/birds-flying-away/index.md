Birds around Anchorage have been seen getting the heck out of here. “I saw a flock of geese in a perfect ‘V’ formation flying south over my house, and it really seemed like they were hauling ass. I guess they don’t want to spend the winter here,” reported a local resident. She continued, “The sandhill cranes left, too. One day they were walking around my neighborhood, seemingly content, and the next day they got out of dodge. I wish I were as mobile as those birds, but I guess I have a life here or something.” An arctic tern soared south overhead.

A homeowner near Westchester Lagoon reported that, “The adult trumpeter swans and their young just up-and-left the other day. It was so nice seeing them in the lagoon on my walks and bike rides, but I think they have the right idea. Who truly would want to stay in Anchorage for the next few months?” The homeowner was tactfully avoiding the use of the ‘W’ word. “I would get the heck away from here if I could.”

At press time, black-capped chickadees were out of their goddamn mind.
