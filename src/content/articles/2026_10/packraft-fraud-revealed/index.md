An investigation found that a premier packraft that retails for over $1000 is actually just a $15 pool floaty in disguise. “I was really disappointed to find out that this premier packraft I bought for basically a month’s rent is really just a pool floaty with a paint job,” stated an outdoorsy resident, deflated. “I thought that I was getting my money’s worth, and that this expensive piece of gear would allow me to go on all sorts of backcountry trips that would otherwise be impossible, but it turns out I could have just gone to Walmart, bought a $15 floating doughnut, installed thigh-straps, and hit the water. But agh they just look so cool!”

At press time, the head of the investigation bought one anyway.
