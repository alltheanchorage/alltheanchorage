An Anchorage resident was seen using their precious time on this beautiful Earth to complain about the Denali Street snow storage project. “While I understand that we are only granted so many seconds on this pale blue dot, and that there are a lot of meaningful endeavors that desperately need more help, I have decided to pour all of my energy into railing as hard as I can against this snow storage pilot. Like they always say: trying new things is bad, experimentation is evil, progress is poison. Give me back my beautiful five-lane Denali Street!”

The project, which reduced the number of lanes on Denali Street from five to three, will speed up plowing operations and provide a snow buffer between pedestrians and the street. The resident however, is hearing none of it. “Hurtling through the cosmos on this insignificant little rock, I contemplate what really matters in life. I think the answer, and what I am going to be remembered for, is my vulgar contribution during the open comment period for the Denali Street snow storage pilot. It feels good to have a purpose!”

The resident was last seen speeding down an empty Denali Street.
