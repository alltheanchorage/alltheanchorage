It is finally that time of year. The sun is setting, the stars are beginning to sparkle, you notice a glimmer of green beginning to twirl in the sky, and then TZZZT TZT TZT BUZZZZ! The Anchorage street lights switch on. The views are ruined.

Every night, the Municipality of Anchorage’s devious plan is executed with clockwork precision as the street lights turn on across the city just as the aurora is teasing her arrival. The street lights emit a haze that covers the town and prevents all locals from getting their deserved feelings of awe with a northern lights viewing. What is the point of living in Alaska if I can’t even enjoy the aurora borealis from the comfort of my SoMiTo basement condo? 

I guess street lights support drivers, walkers, bikers, and skiers, but is it too unreasonable to expect everyone to get their errands done between the 11am and 4pm window of light? I don’t understand the argument of how street lights help with visibility either, as when street lights are on, the aurora is not even visible! Pick an angle and stick with it, Muni!

The one thing that gives me hope in this artificially bright world is that there are other staunch aurora lovers out there. I recently heard that community members are supporting the effort by dismantling street lights one by one along city trails. Keep up the good fight and viva la aurora!

By Lysander Lynx, a long time enjoyer of night vision goggles.
