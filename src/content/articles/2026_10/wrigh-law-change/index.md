Bill Hill, a registered independent, is challenging the incumbent republican, Nick Begich III, for Alaska’s seat in the U.S. House of Representatives. Hill, an educator and fisherman from Bristol Bay, has led his campaign with a strict Alaska First agenda.

He announced at a rally last night that he is considering legally changing his name to ‘Wright Law’.

“My commitment to serving Alaskans first is unwavering, however, maybe changing just my name would convince D.C. of my ability to serve as a representative,” Hill shared at the rally. He went on in detail about how changing his name to ‘Wright Law’ evokes a sense of congressional action that is necessary to bridge the gap between Alaska and the Capitol. Hill, a lifelong Alaskan and supporter of subliminal messaging, wants to convey his congressional agenda more strongly, which includes how he will write bills that will pass into law while he serves on the hill. 

When a rally attendee was asked for her thoughts on Hill’s proposal, she said, “Honestly, I don’t care what he does as long as he lowers the price of gas and makes ‘Good’ his middle name”.
