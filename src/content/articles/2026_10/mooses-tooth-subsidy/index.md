The Alaska Department of Revenue announced that the 2027 Permanent Fund Dividend will be subsidized by profits from Moose’s Tooth, Alaska’s second biggest economy. The report states, “Despite recent increases to the price of a barrel of oil, profits from the oil industry have been steadily declining over the past decade. This means less tax revenue for the state, and of course a smaller PFD payout for Alaskans. To balance out this decline, going forward the PFD will be subsidized by the pizza, beer, and Diablo Sticks sold at Moose’s Tooth Pub and Pizzeria. The numbers don’t lie: resource extraction is on the outs; pizza is the future.”

The report continues, “The ‘Tooth’ restaurants in Anchorage earn more profits than commercial fishing and tourism combined. If you’ve ever eaten at Moose’s Tooth from May to September, this may be intuitive, as you probably parked down the street and waited an hour to be seated. Now, while you’re waiting in line, know that every sale the restaurant makes contributes towards your PFD. We felt it was only right that a highly-profitable private industry that extracts valuable Alaskan resources (e.g., pepperoni) contributes back to the people.”
