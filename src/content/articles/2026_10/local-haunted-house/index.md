For most, the concept of a haunted house conjures up images of zombies, ghosts, and spiderwebs. There might be a serial killer in the closet, or a jumpscare you thought was a statue. A local Anchorage family, however, is doing things a bit differently this Halloween. “We wanted to take things up a notch and put together a haunted house full of things that really terrify Alaskans. That’s why we are featuring scares such as ‘icy sidewalk’, ‘frost on windshield’, and ‘winter tires’. You don’t need zombies to scare Alaskans - we have our own set of terrors!”

“You come around a corner and BOOM!, you’ve slipped on a sidewalk. Realistic and it gets the adrenaline going. Next you’ll see a man holding an ice-scraper chipping away at his windshield - horrifying. Then you’ll have to navigate through a maze of studded tires swinging from the ceiling. We obviously don’t want to spoil everything, but we’re also featuring ‘puffy jacket bursting out of closet’, ‘you’re at a ski swap and know nothing about skis’, and ‘block heaters’. I think we’re really going to scare the snow pants off of Anchorage residents this year!”

The family even set up ‘surprise moose’ in their yard - oh wait that is a real moose.
