Originally sentenced for the crime of ‘not being relevant from May to September in Alaska,’ the board game has done its time in summer jail and is now free to sit motionless on a bookshelf until the next board game night. When asked how it is feeling after five months inside a closet, the board game exclaimed, “No more dust, no more must, it feels great to be out and about again! I’m looking forward to being played for the first time this year, and I live for the puzzled look on people’s faces when they struggle to put all my pieces back inside my box after the game is over.”

Summer jail is not a lonely place, however. “There’s so much company in the closet!” continued the board game. “You’ve got the skis, boots, and poles, who are sort of the cool kids on the block. You’ve got the half-finished sweater, who is quiet at first but really friendly once you get to know it. The ice skates are super punk. The fat tire bike takes up a lot of room but we all love him. We sort of take care of each other, and it’s almost bittersweet to leave, but ultimately I’m glad to be free and sitting on this bookshelf. Here come seven months of glory!”

The board game exchanged a sympathetic look with a paddle as it was gently placed in the closet.
